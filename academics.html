Our Academic Programs

At Good Shepherd High School, we offer a comprehensive curriculum designed to meet the needs of all learners. Our programs combine academic rigor with practical skills development to prepare students for success in higher education and beyond.

Lower Secondary (S1-S4)

Our O-Level program follows the Uganda National Curriculum with emphasis on:

  • Core subjects: English, Mathematics, Sciences
  • Humanities: History, Geography, CRE/IRE
  • Practical skills development
  • ICT integration across all subjects

Upper Secondary (S5-S6)

Our A-Level program offers specialization in three main combinations:

  • Science: PCM, PCB, PEM
  • Arts: HEG, LEA, HGL
  • Business: BAM, BCM, BEM
  • Entrepreneurship training

Special Programs

Enhancing our core curriculum with additional opportunities:

  • STEM Club (Science, Technology, Engineering, Math)
  • Debate and Public Speaking
  • Music, Dance and Drama
  • Sports Academy
  • Career Guidance Program

Curriculum Overview

Our curriculum is designed to develop well-rounded individuals with both academic knowledge and practical life skills.

Level Core Subjects Electives Co-Curricular
Senior 1-2 English, Math, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Geography, History, CRE/IRE French, Luganda, Computer Studies, Literature Sports, Music, Clubs
Senior 3-4 English, Math, 3 Sciences, Geography/History, CRE/IRE Principles of Accounts, Art, ICT Career Guidance, Debating
Senior 5-6 3 Principal Subjects, General Paper, Subsidiary Math/ICT Entrepreneurship, Literature, Art Research Projects, Leadership Training
