Admission Process

We welcome applications from students who demonstrate academic potential, good character, and a willingness to contribute to our school community. Our admission process is designed to be transparent and straightforward.

1

Inquiry

Contact our admissions office or visit our school to learn more about our programs and requirements.

2

Application

Submit the completed application form along with all required documents and application fee.

3

Assessment

Students may be required to take an entrance examination or participate in an interview.

4

Acceptance

Successful applicants will receive an admission letter and payment details.

5

Enrollment

Complete payment and submit all required documents to secure your place.

Admission Requirements

For Senior One Entrants

  • Completed Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) with at least 4 passes
  • Copy of PLE results slip/certificate
  • Birth certificate
  • Three recent passport-sized photographs
  • Transfer letter from previous school (if applicable)
  • Medical report form (provided by the school)

For Other Classes

  • Report cards from previous school
  • Transfer letter
  • Birth certificate
  • Three recent passport-sized photographs
  • Medical report form

International Students

  • Equivalent academic records
  • Valid student visa
  • Passport copies
  • Guardian information
